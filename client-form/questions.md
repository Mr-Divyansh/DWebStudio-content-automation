# D Web Studio Content Automation — Client Intake Questions

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the initial question set for collecting information required for Instagram content generation.

The final form implementation is not decided yet.

---

## 2. Initial Questions

1. What is your business or brand name?
2. What is your business category or industry?
3. What is your website address, if any?
4. What is your Instagram handle, if any?
5. What contact details should appear in the content, if any?
6. Who is your target audience?
7. What is the main goal of this content?
8. What service, product, or event is being promoted?
9. What is your current offer, if any?
10. What is the offer price, if you want it included?
11. What is the offer validity (start/end dates), if applicable?
12. What important information must appear in the content?
13. Is there anything that must not be changed or omitted?
14. What action should the audience take (call to action)?
15. What tone should the content use?
16. What content type do you need (post, Reel, caption, other)?
17. Do you have photos or videos available for this content?
18. Is there anything else relevant we should know?

---

## 3. Data Minimization

- Collect only information needed for content generation.
- Do not collect unnecessary sensitive personal information (for example government IDs, payment details, medical information, or private home details).
- Collect business contact details only when they will be used in the generated content.
