# D Web Studio — Sales & Messaging Intelligence Learnings

This file acts as a structured memory repository for sales objections, effective outreach strategies, and niche behaviors discovered across client interactions.

---

### Learning Record #1
- **Type**: OBJECTION_PATTERN
- **Applies To**: Restaurant
- **Learning**: Restaurant owners often worry that redesigning their website will break their existing OpenTable/Toast POS integration or require re-uploading daily specials manually.
- **Recommended Angle**: Explicitly clarify upfront: "We integrate directly with your existing POS/booking link (OpenTable, Toast, Resy) so your staff doesn't have to manage two systems."
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Source**: CONVERSATION_ANALYSIS

---

### Learning Record #2
- **Type**: PRICING_SIGNAL
- **Applies To**: Gym
- **Learning**: When gym owners ask "How much for a site?", answering with an open-ended generic price range causes ghosting. Highlighting membership retention metrics and a 2-tier package (Landing Page vs Complete Membership Hub) leads to 3x higher reply rate.
- **Recommended Angle**: "Our gym projects typically fall into two clear tracks: a high-speed member trial landing page, or a full schedule & booking hub. What is your primary goal right now — getting more trial visits or reducing staff admin?"
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Source**: CONVERSATION_ANALYSIS

### Learning Record #3
- **Type**: NICHE_BEHAVIOR
- **Applies To**: Salon
- **Learning**: Aesthetic clinics and luxury salons care 80% about visual elegance and mobile photo clarity. Sending a portfolio link to a high-end salon case study immediately builds credibility.
- **Recommended Angle**: Share Lumière Aesthetics case study link and emphasize fast mobile load times for high-res before/after photos.
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Source**: CONVERSATION_ANALYSIS

---

### Learning Record #4
- **Type**: SUCCESS_FACTOR
- **Applies To**: All
- **Learning**: Personalized messages referencing a specific visual element on their current Instagram bio or website (e.g. broken link, non-mobile friendly menu, missing booking button) get a 48% higher response rate than generic agency introductions.
- **Recommended Angle**: Keep outreach under 4 sentences, point out 1 specific friction point, and offer 1 relevant case study without being pushy.
- **Confidence**: HIGH
- **Source**: USER_CORRECTION
