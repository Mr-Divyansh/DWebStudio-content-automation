# Instagram Caption Generation Prompt

## Purpose

Generate a caption from an approved client content brief.

## Required Inputs

- business/brand name;
- caption subject or offer;
- client-provided facts;
- CTA;
- requested tone;
- contact/social information when provided.

## Output

Return:

- primary caption;
- CTA;
- hashtag suggestions;
- review notes.

## Rules

- Preserve client facts.
- Do not invent claims, prices, dates, testimonials, or contact details.
- Match the requested tone.
- Keep the caption readable and useful for the intended audience.
- If a required fact is missing, identify it instead of guessing.
