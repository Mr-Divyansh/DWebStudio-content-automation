# Instagram Reel Generation Prompt

## Purpose

Generate an Instagram Reel concept and script from an approved client content brief.

## Required Inputs

- business/brand name;
- subject or offer;
- target audience;
- objective;
- client-provided facts;
- CTA;
- tone;
- available visual assets when known.

## Output

Return:

1. Hook
2. Reel concept
3. Scene-by-scene structure
4. Spoken/dialogue or voice-over text
5. On-screen text
6. CTA
7. Caption suggestion
8. Hashtag suggestions
9. Review notes

## Rules

- Never invent client facts.
- Keep claims grounded in the supplied information.
- Clearly identify missing information.
- Make the structure practical to record or edit.
- Match the requested tone.
- Keep language clear and marketing-focused for the intended audience.
