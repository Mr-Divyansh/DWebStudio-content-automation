# D Web Studio Content Automation — Design System

## 1. Design Vision

The product should feel:

- simple;
- clear;
- professional;
- practical;
- human-made;
- focused on getting work completed rather than showing unnecessary visual complexity.

---

## 2. Product Experience

The primary experience should reduce friction for clients who are submitting content information.

The interface should make the next action obvious and avoid unnecessary steps.

---

## 3. Information Hierarchy

Important information should appear in this order where applicable:

1. what the client needs to provide;
2. why the information is needed;
3. the action required from the client;
4. generated content and review controls.

---

## 4. Form Design Principles

- Use clear field labels.
- Group related questions together.
- Avoid asking for information that is not used.
- Mark required information clearly.
- Explain fields that could otherwise be ambiguous.
- Keep long forms divided into understandable sections.

---

## 5. Generated Content Presentation

Generated content should be easy to scan and copy.

Where applicable, separate:

- hook;
- main copy;
- CTA;
- hashtags;
- Reel scenes/dialogue;
- notes for review.

---

## 6. Visual Principles (Planned)

- Prefer clarity and whitespace over decoration.
- Keep alignment, spacing, and control styles consistent across steps of the workflow.
- Avoid unnecessary gradients, effects, and visual clutter.
- Mark flagged or missing client information visibly rather than hiding it.

---

## 7. Typography Direction (Planned)

- No typography system has been implemented yet.
- Planned direction: one clean sans-serif family for the interface with a small fixed type scale (body, small, heading, page title).
- Prioritize readability of form labels and generated content over decorative type.
- Final fonts and sizes will be recorded as the source of truth below once implemented.

---

## 8. Spacing Direction (Planned)

- No spacing tokens have been implemented yet.
- Planned direction: a small consistent spacing scale used throughout forms, sections, and generated-content panels.
- Group related form fields visually; separate workflow stages clearly.
- Exact spacing values will be recorded as the source of truth below once implemented.

---

## 9. Component Principles (Planned)

- Keep the initial component set minimal: labeled form field, section header, primary action button, generated-content panel, review/approval control.
- One primary action per step.
- Components must display client-supplied facts exactly as entered and visibly surface flagged/missing information.
- Do not build a component library before the application stack is decided.

---

## 10. Visual Source of Truth

No final color palette, typography system, logo system, or component library has been implemented yet.

When the interface is built, implemented design tokens should be recorded here as the source of truth.

Do not invent a brand palette and treat it as implemented.
