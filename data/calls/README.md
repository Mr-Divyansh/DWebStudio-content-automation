# Call Transcripts Folder

Place customer discovery call transcripts, audio transcripts, or structured notes in this directory.

Supported formats:
- `.txt` plain text speaker transcripts:
  `Speaker 1: Thanks for taking the time today.`
  `Client: Yeah, we have an Italian restaurant and our website is 7 years old.`
- `.vtt` / `.srt` video or audio subtitle transcripts.
- `.json` structured meeting notes.

You can also paste or import call transcripts directly from the Web Dashboard!
